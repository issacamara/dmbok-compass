How do data governance and data quality work together?
⌁
Synthesis · strong evidence

Data governance establishes the decision rights, accountabilities, and policies for managing data. Data quality applies those expectations to make data fit for its intended use. Together, governance defines the standard and quality management measures how consistently the standard is met.

[1] Data Governance · p. 69 · “...the exercise of authority, control, and shared decision-making over the management of data assets.”
[2] Data Quality · p. 331 · “...planning, implementation, and control activities that apply quality management techniques to data.”
